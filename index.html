Course agenda

The session follows the structure itself, from the thick masonry base upward to the frame that replaced it. The rule at the left of each topic thins as the wall does.

  1. 01Overview of early NYC building construction and structural systems
  2. 02Early masonry buildings
  3. 03Growth of NYC and the transition to taller structures
  4. 04Transition from masonry load-bearing walls to steel skeleton frames (cage systems)
  5. 05Cast iron vs. wrought iron
  6. 06How early buildings were designed for lateral loads
  7. 07Influence of the New York City Building Code
